Privacy policy

How the bureau collects, uses and protects your personal data when you use firstlawbureau.com or contact us.

Who is responsible for your data

Law Bureau Sandulieieva LB Consult (Advocate Bureau of Victoria Sanduleeva “Consult”), EDRPOU code 42994071, Kyiv, Ukraine (“the bureau”). For any question about personal data, write to info@firstlawbureau.com.

What data we receive

  • Data you give us yourself: your name, phone or e-mail, messenger handle, country of residence, practice area and a short description of your matter — through the website form, Telegram, WhatsApp or when you book a consultation.
  • Technical data without your name: the request number, the page you contacted us from, where you came from (for example Google or LinkedIn), your country based on IP address, the date and time.
  • Anonymous visit statistics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) — no cookies and no cross-site tracking.

Why we use it

  • To answer your request, book your consultation and, if you decide to go ahead, conclude an agreement for legal assistance.
  • To understand which pages and channels help people find the bureau, and to improve the website.

Legal basis

The Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection” and, for clients in the EU, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR): steps taken at your request before entering into an agreement (Art. 6(1)(b)), performance of the agreement, compliance with an advocate’s legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)) and the bureau’s legitimate interest in anonymous statistics (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Advocate–client privilege

Everything you tell the bureau in connection with legal assistance is protected by advocate–client privilege. Only the bureau’s advocates and legal counsel working on your matter have access to your requests. Names, contact details and the content of requests are never passed to third parties for advertising or marketing.

Who may receive your data

  • Technical services that run the website and communications: Cloudflare (website hosting, e-mail, request database), Google (booking calendar, Google Meet, e-mail), Telegram and WhatsApp — if you choose that channel yourself.
  • Public authorities — only where the law expressly requires it.
  • These services may process data outside the EU and Ukraine, including in the United States, under their own data protection safeguards.

How long we keep data

Requests that do not lead to an agreement — up to 12 months. Client data — for the term of the agreement and for the period that the law requires an advocate to keep case files. Anonymous statistics are kept without a time limit.

Cookies

The website uses no advertising or tracking cookies. To record where a request came from, your browser temporarily keeps a technical entry (sessionStorage) that disappears when you close the tab.

Your rights

You may ask for access to your data, its correction or deletion, restriction of processing and data portability, and you may object to processing. Write to info@firstlawbureau.com — we reply within 30 days. You may also complain to the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights or to the data protection authority of your EU country.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version, with its date, is always published on this page.
