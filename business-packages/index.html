Transparent terms

The monthly allowance covers the total time spent on included legal work — including consultations, document review, drafting and correspondence. Document counts are maximums for standard documents and do not replace the time allowance.

Typically billed separately or subject to a separate engagement: court appearances and litigation; extensive or complex transactions; urgent on-site attendance; specialised tax or accounting work; third-party fees, filing fees, notary, translation and other external costs; and work beyond the plan allowance.

Unused hours do not roll over unless agreed in writing.

All fees and terms

Good to know

What counts toward my monthly hours?

All time spent on included legal work, including consultations, reviewing or drafting documents, research and correspondence.

Are the document limits separate from the hour allowance?

They are an additional cap on the number of standard documents. Both the document limit and the total monthly hours apply.

Can I request work beyond my plan?

Yes. Additional work can be scoped and billed separately, but we will confirm the scope and fees before proceeding.

Do unused hours carry over?

No, unless a rollover arrangement is expressly agreed in writing.

Does the subscription include court representation?

Court appearances, litigation and other substantial proceedings are not included by default and require a separate agreement.

Can I change plans?

Plan changes can be discussed based on your business needs and take effect as agreed in writing.
