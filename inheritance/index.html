Inheritance and estates

If an estate is being administered in Ukraine while you live abroad, we help identify the steps, deadlines and documents that apply to your case. We assess what can be done through a representative and what may require your involvement or a notarial act.

Who we help

  • Foreign nationals who inherit property or funds in Ukraine
  • Heirs who live abroad and cannot travel
Inheritance

What we help with

  • Acceptance of inheritance

    We file your application with a Ukrainian notary while you are abroad and keep track of deadlines.

  • Documentation of inherited property rights

    We gather the documents for an apartment, house, land or accounts and obtain the certificate of inheritance.

  • Missed deadlines

    We explain whether a missed deadline can be restored and prepare the court application.

  • Wills

    We check whether a will is valid and explain how it is carried out in Ukraine.

  • Disputes between heirs

    We represent you in negotiations and in court when heirs cannot agree.
